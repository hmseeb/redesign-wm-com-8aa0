City of Los Angeles partnership

What is recycLA?

The City of Los Angeles launched recycLA, a public-private partnership that, for the first time in the history of the City, offers customer-friendly and efficient waste and recycling collection services to commercial/industrial businesses and large multifamily buildings.

The recycLA program will help move the City closer to achieving its zero waste goals through its pioneering waste reduction, reuse, recycling, and recovery programs.

Program benefits

  • Clean fuel collection vehicles
  • Recycling at 100% of customer sites
  • Annual landfill reduction targets of one million tons
  • Recycling infrastructure investment and development
  • Standardized and transparent rates
  • Customizable waste and recycling services
  • 24/7 customer service support
  • Enforcement of customer service standards

Organics

Food scraps, food-soiled paper and yard trimmings belong in your organics container. Review the recycLA organics compliance requirements so your site meets its obligations, and post the bilingual guidelines where your staff sorts material.

Edible surplus food can be donated instead of discarded — the recycLA Food Rescue Program connects businesses and non-profits to keep good food out of the waste stream.
